Heads up — browser wording

Your browser will say "Upload". Nothing actually uploads.

Safari and Firefox don't yet support the modern folder-access API. They fall back to a file-input prompt that's labelled "Upload N files to this site".

Better MD viewer ignores that wording. Your files are read into this tab and rendered locally. Nothing is sent over the network — you can confirm in your browser's Network tab.
